Content Studio Tools

Content Studio Tools is a private set of scripts that one person uses to manage his own YouTube channels: uploading and scheduling his own Shorts and reading his own channel analytics. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

What data the app accesses

  • With your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to upload videos to your own channel, edit their titles, descriptions and scheduling, and read your channel's statistics (views, retention, likes, subscribers).
  • It does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is only used to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to analyse the performance of his own channels.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer, in files readable only by him. Nothing is stored on any server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How to revoke access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For any question about this app, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
